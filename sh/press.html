Press kit · A cozy pixel-art astronomy game about the real night sky. Out now on Google Play; coming soon to the App Store.

Every star, planet, nebula and galaxy over your hill is real, and shines where it really is tonight. Stargazer's Hill is a cozy pixel-art game about our real night sky: look up and name the stars with your own eyes, find wonders with the Sky Guide, look through your telescope, and photograph what you find, its true colours developing in the camera. Start with an old pair of binoculars in your backyard and work your way up to big telescopes under some of the darkest skies on Earth, from La Palma to the Atacama Desert and the northern lights at Tromsø.

What's in it

  • The real sky: over 5,000 real stars in their true colours and places, all 88 constellations and the Milky Way
  • About 700 things to find, each with real facts: all 110 Messier objects, showpieces of both hemispheres, faint galaxies and quasars
  • The solar system as it really moves: the planets, the Moon's phases, Jupiter's moons where they really are, real comets
  • Telescopes that behave like telescopes, from binoculars to a 24-inch observatory: aperture, eyepieces, mounts, cameras and filters
  • Photograph what you find, graded, and sell the best to Night Sky Monthly
  • Real dark-sky sites: your own backyard, Cherry Springs, La Palma, Tromsø's northern lights, the Atacama, Lake Tekapo
  • Share your photos as cards or a short looping clip with music
  • 37 quests, 129 achievements, a logbook, the Star Radio's 31 calm songs, and red light mode
  • Sky events on the real calendar, like the Geminid meteor shower in December
