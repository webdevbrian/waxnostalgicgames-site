Press kit · Mow perfect stripes and grow a cozy lawn care business, one tidy yard at a time.

A relaxing isometric lawn care tycoon in pixel art. Start with a hand-me-down push mower and the lawns on your own street: every pass lays down its own stripe, the neighbors call with jobs, and each tidy yard brings pay, a tip and a five-star review. Hire a crew and watch them mow stripes of their own, buy a truck and paint your logo on the doors, and grow across eight places to work, from Maple Street to the country club. Free in spring and summer; the Full Game adds fall's leaves, winter's snow and two more places.

What's in it

  • Mow perfect stripes: every pass lays down its own, and Lanes steering keeps them crisp and straight
  • Trim, sweep, power wash drives, paint fences and shape bushes; with the Full Game, rake leaves and clear snow
  • Hire a crew from the job board, each with their own skills and quirks, and watch them work
  • Gear up at Hank's Hardware, from a self-propelled mower to a zero-turn, and build a fleet of five trucks in your colors
  • Eight places to work, from Maple Street to the Fairway Glen Country Club (two with the Full Game)
  • A neighborhood that's alive: squirrels, robins, fireflies at dusk, dog walkers and a raccoon in the trash cart
  • A metal detector and 144 things to find in the grass, from a top set of dentures to a dinosaur tooth
  • Eight radio stations, 104 original songs, 90 achievements, and an idle mode
  • Seasonal events: the Harvest Festival in November, Holiday Lights from 1 to 26 December
