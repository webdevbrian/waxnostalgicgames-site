Press kit · Fire a rocket into the sky, dodge everything on the way up, grab everything on the way down, then blast your haul for cash.

A pixel-art arcade game about the round trip. Tilt to steer your rocket up through birds, balloons, planes, satellites and UFOs; the first thing you hit stalls you. Then flip nose-down and fall back to Earth, tractor-beaming everything you touch, and fling the whole haul over the launch pad to shoot it for cash. Five skies from sleepy Pinecrest to the Moon, a giant boss above each one, seven guns and thirty challenges. Version 1.5 adds a seasonal event every month.

What's in it

  • Dodge up: tilt to steer, hold to boost, and the first thing you hit stalls the rocket
  • Grab down: fall nose-first and tractor-beam the sky, with a parachute to line up the big catches
  • Blast it all: fling the haul over the pad and shoot it for cash, with a Clean Sweep bonus for getting everything (but not the jellies)
  • Five skies and five bosses: the Sky Whale, the Space Kraken, the Aurora Dragon, the Mothership and the Cosmic Goose
  • Seven guns with their own mods, fuel tanks, shields, boosters, slow-mo and paint jobs
  • Coming in 1.5, a seasonal event every month: a runaway sleigh in December, fireworks in January, heart balloons in February, kites in March, each with a paint job to win
