Press kit · Skip stones across still water, from dawn to starry night. Then launch one all the way to space.

A relaxing pixel-art stone-skipping game. Swipe from the dock to throw, tap just as the stone kisses the water for a perfect skip, and watch the light follow your clock from soft sunrise to starry night with fireflies. Skip all the way across and the stone keeps going, over the land, through the fence, and, with a Sling Shot, a Stone Cannon and a Stone Rocket, all the way to space. Sixteen spots, a dog or cat on the dock, and over a hundred creatures to photograph.

What's in it

  • Swipe to throw, tap on the touchdown for a perfect skip, and string skips into a streak
  • Lily pads, sleepy turtles, drifting logs and friendly seals to bounce off; a breeze or a gull to help
  • Across and beyond: tumble over the land, knock over gnomes and hay bales, kick the stone on
  • The Sling Shot, the Stone Cannon and the Stone Rocket, up past kites and the jet stream to space
  • Sixteen spots, from Meadow Creek's mill pond to the stormy Devil's Triangle
  • Real time of day and weather: sunrises, rain, morning fog, snow, and fireflies at night
  • A dog or cat on the dock (you can pet them), a nature journal of over 100 finds, legends like a yeti and a werewolf
  • Ninety-three challenges, sixty hats, outfits and trails, thirty gentle songs, and an idle mode
