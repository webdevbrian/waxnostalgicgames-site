Press kit · Flick a ball up the lane, light up the machine, win tickets, buy ridiculous prizes. Then go again.

A silly, loud pixel-art arcade lane. Flick a ball into the rings, set off the machine's lights, and win paper tickets to spend at the prize counter on ridiculous junk that makes you better: a kazoo, a rubber chicken, someone else's trophy. Six lanes to earn (a pizza parlor, a boardwalk, a roller rink) and eleven more in packs, 31 balls (a whole potato, a meatball, a black hole), a jukebox, visitors wandering round the machine, and your best ball replayed like a sports broadcast, ready to share.

What's in it

  • Rounds of nine balls on an old arcade lane with a pinball brain: rollovers, a hot ring, combos, multiball, a jackpot
  • A slow-motion last ball, and instant replays shot like a sports broadcast
  • A prize counter of 74 silly prizes that each change the game: a kazoo, a rubber chicken, someone else's trophy
  • 31 balls to roll, from a whole potato to a black hole
  • Seven free lanes (a pizza parlor, a boardwalk, a bowling alley reject, a roller rink…), each with its own animated backboard, songs and visitors
  • Three packs of eleven stranger lanes: Lights Out, Creature Feature and Bad Ideas
  • A jukebox of the game's own songs, and a share card or clip of your best ball
