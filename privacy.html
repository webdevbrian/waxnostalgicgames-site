The short version

Wax Nostalgic Games doesn't collect information about you. Our games don't have accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and our website doesn't use cookies or analytics.

Our games

Each game has its own privacy policy, linked from its page and its store listing. As of today, none collects any data: your progress and settings stay on your device. Ramp n Roll's privacy policy.

Our marketing tools

We use a small private tool, "Wax Nostalgic Marketing", to publish our own posts, videos and email drafts to our own accounts (for example our YouTube channel and our Gmail). It's used only by Wax Nostalgic Games, signs in only to accounts we own, and doesn't access, collect or share anyone else's data. When it uses Google services, it only uploads our videos, reads our own channel's details, and writes drafts in our own mailbox; nothing it receives from Google is shared with anyone or used for anything else, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply to you, and don't share them.

Children

We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions: waxnostalgicgames@gmail.com.
